From the first photo to the final gallery

A simple 5-step process

Plan

Choose the style, locations, outfits and types of photos before shooting.

Shoot

Use simple composition, positioning, angles and lighting techniques.

Select

Choose the strongest photos and remove images that weaken your gallery.

Edit

Apply simple edits that enhance the image without looking artificial.

Organize

Arrange your final selection so the gallery feels intentional and balanced.

Your phone can be enough

No expensive camera required

You don’t need a professional photographer to start. With the right techniques, your smartphone can capture clean, polished, professional-looking photos. The guide focuses on practical techniques you can apply with the equipment you already have.

Before the shoot

  • Choose your visual style
  • Select locations
  • Plan outfits
  • Prepare your shot list
  • Check lighting conditions

During the shoot

  • Capture different angles
  • Vary your distance from the camera
  • Try different compositions
  • Take close-up and wider shots
  • Capture natural-looking poses

After the shoot

  • Select your strongest images
  • Remove repetitive photos
  • Edit consistently
  • Adjust colors and lighting
  • Arrange your final gallery

A path in four stages

How the plan
is organized

A monthly sequence that starts with the foundations and leaves room to review and plan again. The 30 days describe the learning plan, not a deadline for results.

  1. 01

    Week 1 / Foundations

    Positioning, profile review with the checklist and topic selection.

  2. 02

    Week 2 / Routine

    Prepare your first posts with the templates and set up the editorial calendar.

  3. 03

    Week 3 / Formats

    Try short video, carousel and still-image formats around the same topic.

  4. 04

    Week 4 / Review

    Review your own numbers with the metrics guide and plan the following month.
